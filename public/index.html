Last Light keeps the things your family would need — accounts, documents, instructions, a few words — locked until the day you stop answering. Then it opens, only for the people you chose.

Set up my vault Source code

1. Fill your vault

Everything is encrypted in your browser before it's saved. This server only ever stores locked data it can't read.

2. Choose Lightkeepers

Pick 2 to 10 people. Each gets a Keeper Card. Any few of them together — you decide how many — can open the vault. Nobody can alone.

3. Send your signal

Every month we ask, "still here?" One click and that's it. Miss it, and we remind you — many times — before telling anyone.

4. Last Light

Only after a long silence are your Lightkeepers sent a link. They combine their cards and see what you left for them.

Why you can trust it

Your vault key is split with Shamir's secret sharing. The server holds the pieces locked; your Lightkeepers hold the keys to those locks. The server can't open anything, your Lightkeepers can't open anything early, and the code is open source so anyone can check.

Last Light is open-source software you run on your own hosting.
